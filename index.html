Features

Everything built in

No mod hunting, no setup. Open the launcher, pick a version and play.

40+ Mods

Toggle every mod from one clean menu. Just press Right Shift in game.

FPS Boost

Sodium is built in, so you get smoother frames on any PC.

Minimap & Waypoints

Always know where you are and never lose your base again.

Zoom & Keystrokes

Smooth zoom, keystrokes display and the HUD tools PvP players love.

3D Clothes

Hats, jackets and more in full 3D. Every Quake player can see them.

Your Own Capes

Emotes

Host for Friends

Get started

Playing in 3 steps

Download

Get DowyClient-Setup.exe with the Download button above.

Install

Run DowyClient-Setup.exe. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway.

Play

Choose a version, hit Play and press Right Shift for the mod menu.

FAQ

Questions

Is Quake Client free?

Yes. Quake Client is completely free to download and use.

Do I need to own Minecraft?

Yes. You need a Minecraft: Java Edition account to play.

Which Minecraft versions work?

1.21.6, 1.21.7, 1.21.8, 1.21.9, 1.21.10, 1.21.11 and 26.3. You choose the version inside the launcher.

How do I update?

You don't have to download again. The launcher finds new versions by itself and shows a Restart & update button.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

Right now Quake Client is made for Windows 10 and 11.

Where can I get help?

Join our Discord server and ask there.
